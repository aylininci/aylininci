Approach

A systematic process from analysis to measurement

In SEO and web development, what matters is not just listing existing issues, but evaluating them by impact and turning them into applicable solutions.

01

Analysis

The site's technical infrastructure, content structure, organic performance and user experience are examined together.

Decisions are shaped by existing data, crawl results and real user behavior rather than assumptions.

02

Prioritization

Findings are classified by their potential impact, implementation requirements and business goals.

Critical technical issues, content gaps that limit visibility, and problems affecting user experience are addressed first.

03

Implementation

Technical adjustments, content improvements and necessary code changes are turned into applicable tasks.

Where needed, changes are applied directly or carried out in coordination with the relevant technical and content teams.

04

Measurement

The effects on organic traffic, impressions, clicks, rankings, user behavior and conversion performance are tracked.

New priorities and improvement areas are defined based on the results.
