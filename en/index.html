From analysis to measurement

    
Analysis
The site's technical infrastructure, content structure, organic performance and user experience are examined together. Decisions are shaped by existing data, crawl results and real user behavior rather than assumptions.
Prioritization
Findings are classified by their potential impact, implementation requirements and business goals. Critical technical issues are addressed first.
Implementation
Technical adjustments, content improvements and code changes are broken down into tasks. These tasks are applied directly or carried out with the relevant teams.
Measurement
The effects on organic traffic, impressions, clicks, rankings and conversion performance are tracked. New priorities are set based on the results.
